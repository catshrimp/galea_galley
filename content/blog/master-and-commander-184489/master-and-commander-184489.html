Список кораблей английского флота по состоянию на 1643 год

Источник списка – первый том книги известного английского исседователя Гранвилла Пенна (Granville Penn), внука адмирала Уильяма Пенна. Книга называется Memorials of the Professional Life and Times of Sir William Penn, Knt., Admiral and General of the Fleet, During the Interregnum; Admiral, and Commissioner of the Admiralty and Navy, after the Restoration. From 1644 To 1670 (vol.I, pp. 66-68)

A List of His Majesty’s Navy Royal and Merchant
Ships, their Names, Captains, Men, Ordnance in every
Ship, now set forth for the Guard of the Narrow Seas,
and for the Coast of Ireland, in this present Expedition,
1643. Under the Command of the Right Hon. Robert
Earl of Warwick, Admiral and Commander-in-chief of
the said Fleet.
